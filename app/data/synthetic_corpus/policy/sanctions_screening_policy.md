# Sanctions Screening Policy

**Document type:** Policy
**Status:** Synthetic placeholder — illustrative only, not real compliance guidance.

## Purpose

This policy sets out how the organization screens customers,
counterparties, and transactions against applicable sanctions and
watchlists before onboarding and on an ongoing basis.

## Screening Points

Screening occurs at four points: (1) at onboarding, before any account is
activated; (2) on a recurring batch basis against updated list versions;
(3) at the time of any material change to customer or beneficial-owner
information; and (4) in real time for outbound and inbound payment
instructions where the organization's systems support transaction-level
screening.

## Scope of Screening

Screening covers the customer, any joint account holders, beneficial
owners identified through the KYC process, and named counterparties on
transactions. Name-matching uses fuzzy matching to account for
transliteration and formatting differences, which produces some false
positives by design — precision is intentionally traded for recall on
this control.

## Handling Potential Matches

A potential match does not itself confirm a true match. A trained
reviewer compares available identifying details — date of birth,
nationality, address, associated entities — against the listed party
before escalating. Where a match cannot be confidently excluded, the
transaction or account activity is held pending further review, and the
matter is escalated to compliance leadership.

## True Matches

Where a match is confirmed, the organization does not proceed with the
transaction or relationship and follows the internal blocking/rejection
procedure, which includes notifying the appropriate compliance function
and, where required, the relevant authority within applicable timeframes.

## List Maintenance

Sanctions and watchlist data feeds are updated on a defined schedule, and
any gap in update processing is logged and escalated as an operational
incident, since a stale list creates a screening blind spot.

## Recordkeeping

Screening results, reviewer decisions, and escalations are retained per
the Recordkeeping & Retention Policy, including for potential matches
that were ultimately cleared.
