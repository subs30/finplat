# Know Your Customer (KYC) Policy Summary

**Document type:** Policy
**Status:** Synthetic placeholder — illustrative only, not real compliance guidance.

## Purpose

This policy establishes the minimum standards for verifying the identity
of customers before onboarding and throughout the customer relationship.
It applies to all new individual and business accounts opened by the
organization, and to existing accounts undergoing periodic review.

## Identity Verification Requirements

Before an account may be activated, the organization must collect and
verify: full legal name, date of birth (for individuals) or registration
details (for entities), current residential or registered address, and a
government-issued identification number. Verification should rely on
reliable, independent source documents or data, not solely on customer
self-attestation.

For business customers, the organization must additionally identify and
verify the beneficial owner(s) — any individual who ultimately owns or
controls 25% or more of the entity, or who otherwise exercises control
over its management. Layered corporate structures should be traced back
to a natural person wherever practicable.

## Ongoing Monitoring

KYC is not a one-time event. Customer information must be refreshed on a
risk-based schedule: high-risk customers reviewed at least annually,
medium-risk customers every two to three years, and low-risk customers on
a longer cycle. A material change in a customer's business activity,
ownership, or transaction pattern should trigger an off-cycle review
regardless of the scheduled date.

## Escalation

Where identity cannot be adequately verified, or where a customer is
unwilling to provide required information, the account should not be
opened, or an existing relationship should be escalated for a decision on
continued service. Escalations are logged and reviewed by a designated
compliance reviewer.

## Recordkeeping

Copies of identification documents and verification results are retained
for the duration of the relationship plus a minimum retention period
after account closure, consistent with the organization's Recordkeeping &
Retention Policy.
