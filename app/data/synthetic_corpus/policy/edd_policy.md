# Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) Policy

**Document type:** Policy
**Status:** Synthetic placeholder — illustrative only, not real compliance guidance.

## Purpose

This policy defines when enhanced due diligence is required beyond
standard KYC, and what additional steps that diligence involves.

## Triggers for EDD

Enhanced due diligence is required for: customers rated high-risk under
the Customer Risk Rating Policy, customers with material connections to
jurisdictions considered higher-risk for money laundering or terrorist
financing, politically exposed persons and their immediate family or
close associates, customers whose ownership structure involves multiple
layers of legal entities across jurisdictions, and any customer flagged
by a prior suspicious activity escalation that did not result in
relationship termination but warrants closer ongoing attention.

## Additional Steps

Where EDD is triggered, the organization gathers and documents: the
source of the customer's wealth and the source of funds expected to flow
through the account, a clearer picture of the beneficial ownership chain
including any intermediate holding entities, the business rationale for
any unusually complex structure, and independent corroboration of stated
business activity (e.g., through public filings, media checks, or
third-party data) rather than relying solely on customer-provided
information.

## Approval

Onboarding a customer who requires EDD, or continuing a relationship with
an existing customer who becomes subject to EDD, requires sign-off from a
compliance officer above a defined seniority level, not just the
frontline relationship team.

## Ongoing EDD Monitoring

Customers subject to EDD receive more frequent transaction reviews and a
lower threshold for escalating unusual activity than standard-risk
customers. The rationale for continuing the relationship is re-documented
at each periodic review, rather than assumed to still hold from the prior
review.

## Exiting the Relationship

Where EDD reveals that a customer's actual activity is materially
inconsistent with their stated purpose, and no satisfactory explanation
is obtained, the relationship is escalated for a decision on restricting
services or exiting the customer, following the organization's account
closure procedures.
