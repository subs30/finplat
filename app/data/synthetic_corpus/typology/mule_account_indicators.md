# Typology: Mule Account Indicators

**Document type:** Typology
**Status:** Synthetic placeholder — illustrative only, not real compliance guidance.

## Description

A mule account is a legitimate-looking account, often opened by a real
person, that is used to receive and forward illicit funds on behalf of a
third party — sometimes knowingly, sometimes because the account holder
was deceived (e.g., a "work from home" or romance-scam recruitment) into
allowing their account to be used. Mule accounts are a key mechanism for
moving fraud proceeds and layering funds while keeping the true
beneficiary at a distance from the account.

## Common Patterns

- An account with a long history of low, ordinary activity that suddenly
  begins receiving multiple incoming transfers from unrelated
  third-party senders, followed quickly by outgoing transfers to a
  different set of parties.
- Funds received and forwarded within a very short window (often same
  day), with the account holder retaining little or none of the funds.
- Account holder profile inconsistent with the transaction volume — e.g.,
  a student or low-income account suddenly moving amounts far exceeding
  their declared income.
- Multiple unrelated mule accounts that all send funds onward to the same
  small set of consolidation accounts.
- Login or device patterns inconsistent with the account holder's usual
  behavior (e.g., sudden access from unfamiliar devices or locations),
  which can indicate the account is being directed by someone other than
  its nominal holder.

## Red Flags

- Sudden pass-through behavior in an otherwise dormant or low-activity
  account.
- Incoming funds from multiple, seemingly unrelated senders with no
  plausible common business relationship to the account holder.
- Account holder unable to coherently explain the source or purpose of
  incoming funds when asked.
- Pattern consistent with recruitment via social media or messaging
  platforms promising easy payment for "helping move money."

## Investigative Approach

Look for the pass-through signature — funds in, funds out, minimal
residual balance, short dwell time — rather than any single large
transaction. Comparing the account holder's stated profile against
observed transaction volume is usually the fastest way to distinguish a
genuine mule account from ordinary elevated activity.
